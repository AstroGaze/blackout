# Blackout

A browser extension that hides content which could spoil a piece of media the user hasn't finished yet.

## Language

### Topics and terms

**Protected Topic**:
A piece of media (game, film, series, book, or anything else) that the user wants to avoid spoilers for. GTA 6 is one example, not a special case.
_Avoid_: Game, title, show, watchlist item

**Related Term**:
A word or phrase tied to a Protected Topic (another name for it, a character, place, faction or event) that reveals content is about that topic even when the topic's name is absent. Most are generated by AI; the user may also add their own.
_Avoid_: Keyword, tag, filter word

**Strong Term**:
A Related Term unique enough to the Protected Topic that it identifies the topic on its own (e.g. "Leonida").
_Avoid_: Exact match, primary keyword

**Weak Term**:
A Related Term that is also an everyday word or name (e.g. "Jason"), so it only identifies the topic alongside another Related Term or in a context about that topic.
_Avoid_: Fuzzy match, secondary keyword

**Spoiler Level**:
How much a Related Term gives away by itself (Common knowledge, Minor or Major), so the user can look at the term list from least to most revealing. Separate from whether the term is Strong or Weak.
_Avoid_: Severity, risk, rating

### Protection

**Strict Mode**:
The rule that everything related to a Protected Topic is hidden, whether or not it actually reveals anything, including content that only implies the topic. Strictness wins over convenience.
_Avoid_: Blackout (that's the product), spoiler filter, spoiler-only mode

**Hidden Item**:
A single piece of page content (a video, post, comment) that the extension has covered, along with the reason it was hidden.
_Avoid_: Blocked post, spoiler

**Pause**:
A temporary break in protection for a Protected Topic that switches back on by itself after a set time.
_Avoid_: Disable, snooze

### Sources

**Blocked Source**:
One account on one site (a YouTube channel, subreddit, Reddit user or X account) whose content is always hidden, whatever it appears to be about.
_Avoid_: Blacklist, perpetrator, spoiler account

**Suggested Block**:
A prompt to make a source a Blocked Source, offered after its content has repeatedly become Hidden Items for an active Protected Topic. Never applied without the user's click.
_Avoid_: Auto-block, recommendation
