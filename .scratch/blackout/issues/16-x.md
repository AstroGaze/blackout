## Comments

Implemented: an X site adapter (`src/sites/x/adapter.ts`), a content script for `*://x.com/*` (twitter.com only redirects there), and a "Block this account" right-click entry.

**Two X apps:**
- **Signed in:** X still serves its older app, with `data-testid` hooks, in the user's language (the user's is Spanish).
- **Logged out:** X serves a newer app from 2026, built with Tailwind classes and no `data-testid` at all. It shows only single posts and profiles; the timeline, search and explore need sign-in.
- **What works in both:** the adapter relies on what the two share:
  - every post is an `article`
  - account links are `/<handle>`
  - post links are `/<handle>/status/<id>`
  - a post's own text is in `dir="auto"` blocks

  It never relies on English text.

**What is checked:**
- **Posts everywhere:** the timeline, a post's page and its replies, search results, profiles and notifications. A quoted post sits inside the post quoting it, so it's checked with it.
- **Logged out:** replies and suggested accounts come wrapped in a link (`[role="link"][data-href]`), and that wrapper is what gets covered.
- **Beside the timeline:**
  - trends (`trend`)
  - suggested accounts (`UserCell`), with their names and bios
  - the explore page's featured event (`eventHero`)
  - the "Live on X" broadcast
  - the card that opens over an account's name (`HoverCard`)
- **The search box:** every row of its dropdown (`[id^="typeaheadDropdown"] [role="option"]`). That covers suggested searches and accounts while typing, and your recent searches before you type. Recent searches carry no test ids.

**Pages:**
- **A post's page** (`/<handle>/status/<id>`, `/i/status/<id>`, and its photo or video viewer) is the page's subject, so it gets the full-page cover with the tab title masked. The title quotes the post.
- **Only the current post:** the subject is trusted only once an `article` linking to that post is on the page, since after navigating the previous post can linger. Its quotes and reposts lists aren't a single post.
- **A profile is a page subject too**, like a subreddit's own pages on Reddit:
  - **What's checked:** its name, handle and bio, which are everything above the profile's tabs.
  - **Lingering header:** the header must name the address's handle, since the previous profile's can linger.
  - **No tabs:** an account with no tabs (it doesn't exist, is suspended, or has blocked the user) is checked by what its page says.

**Sources:**
- **Order:** an item's sources are its author first, then whoever reposted it (`socialContext`), then a quoted post's author. X draws the quoted author without links, so it's read from the picture's `UserAvatar-Container-<handle>`.
- **Blocking:** "Block source" on a cover offers the author. Blocking any of an item's sources hides it.
- **Mentions:** accounts mentioned in a post's text, or in a bio, don't count, because those are inside `dir="auto"`. On a live replies tab, 16 posts named no other account outside that text. X no longer shows "Replying to @x" lines there.
- **Stored form:** accounts are stored as `@<handle>` in lowercase, and shown as X shows them.
- **Counting:** a post and its page share the key `status/<id>`, so a post viewed as a page counts once towards Suggested Blocks.

The user's handle and name are replaced with `blackout_user` / "Blackout User", and "followers you know" and pinned timeline tabs are removed. The empty search box with recent searches was captured but not saved, since it's the user's history; a hand-built row in the same shape tests it.

- **How captures reached the disk:** the page put each one, gzipped, on the clipboard, and PowerShell saved it. X's content security policy blocks sending to localhost, and messages to a receiver window never arrived.

**Checked live in headless Brave, logged out, with the GTA 6 preset:**

- **@RockstarGames:** the profile is shown, its 3 GTA 6 posts are covered, and a Suggested Block for @RockstarGames is offered.
- **@GTASixInfo:** covered as a whole, as its name and bio are about GTA 6.
- **No page errors.** Signed-in X couldn't be checked live with Blackout, since Claude in Chrome has no Blackout installed. The signed-in snapshots stand in for it.

**Two-axis review, fixed:**
- **Lingering profile:** a lingering previous profile header could be checked under the new address.
- **Lost sources:** a `dir="auto"` above an item dropped all its sources; the check now stops at the item.
- **Mismatched keys:** absolute and `/i/web/status/` links weren't read the same way as the page's address, so one post could count twice.
- **Stuck pages:** quotes and reposts lists of a post, and profiles without tabs, would have stayed pending and ended on the "couldn't check" cover.
- **Tidying:** `distinctSources` is now shared with Reddit, the builders moved to `fixtures/post.ts`, and twitter.com was dropped.

**Decisions for the user:**
- **Profiles as page subjects:** the ticket asks for single posts. Covering a whole profile whose name or bio is about a topic follows the subreddit decision in ticket 15.
- **Continuing past a post's cover:** the post itself stays covered as an item, so seeing it takes a second Reveal. Reddit threads behave the same way. Revealing the page's own post on Continue would be a small change to the shared guard.

**Not done:**
- **Pictures:** profile banners and media can show GTA 6 art. The blur-all-thumbnails switch is YouTube only.
- **Signed in, live:** notifications, DMs, Communities and Lists weren't captured. Posts there are `article`s too.
- **Text inside media:** text in videos and images isn't read, and neither is alt text.
